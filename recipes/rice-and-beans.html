Rice and Beans

Description

Today you are goiing to learn how to prepare the worst rice and beans of your life! It haves rice, and also have beans, for our surprise. The bean broth all watery, and cracking rices!!:)

Ingredients

  • rice
  • beans
  • water
  • salt
  • oil
  • pan

Steps

  1. boil the water in the pan
  2. add the beans and let it cook for 10 minutes
  3. add the rice and let it cook for 10 minutes
  4. put in the plate
  5. eat the oil and the salt that you got to the recipe
