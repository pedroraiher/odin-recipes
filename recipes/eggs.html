Eggs

Description

Today you are goiing to learn how to prepare the best egg of your life! It haves egg. Nothing else. Accept it, this is our fate.

Ingredients

  • egg
  • water
  • pan

Steps

  1. boil the water in the pan
  2. put the egg into the boiling water
  3. wait about 8-12 mins
  4. eject the egg from the pan
  5. wait it naturally cooling
  6. open the egg
  7. if the egg is ok, eat it
  8. if the egg is rotten, don't eat it
